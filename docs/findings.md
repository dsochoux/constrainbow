   **Agreed design (2026-11-15), to be tuned by play-testing:**

   Experiments (2026-11-15, against `words.txt`):
   - **Pre-filled letters work best.** Starting from an ordinary speed layout (10 colored tiles) and a random answer, filling in black tiles from that answer makes it unique after a median of 4 letters (range 3–6, out of 30 puzzles). One puzzle also needed a colored tile revealed.
   - **Blank boards can be unique**, but only with 13–14 of the 20 tiles colored: about 3% of random 4-color layouts at 13 tiles and 1.5% at 14. Most layouts at 14+ have no solution at all. Finding them is cheap (600 layouts in about 2 s), and the answers are often normal words (THETA / INANE / ATOLL / ENACT), sometimes obscure (EXEAT, ILIAC).
   - **Answer-first also works.** Pick 4 words, then choose 4 letters that each appear at least twice in them as the colors. The layout is then forced, because every place a color's letter appears must carry that color (a black tile can never hold a color's letter). Over 40 random word sets, the best choice of colors gave 8–13 colored tiles but was never unique alone (0 of 1,000 random word sets; 91% still had over 1,000 solutions). It needed a median of 5 pre-filled letters (range 2–7), and 4 sets couldn't be made unique with black-tile letters at all. This costs about one more letter than layout-first, in exchange for choosing the answer (common words, themed days).
   - **A 5th color doesn't help**: 1% unique at 14 tiles, and about 10× slower to search.
   - **Uniqueness depends on the word list.** It has to be checked against every word the game *accepts*, or players will find a second valid answer. This conflicts with feature 6 unless uniqueness is checked against the bigger list and the answer itself uses only common words.

## Accounts and a friends scoreboard (designed 2026-11-23; built on branch `accounts-friends`, not deployed)
*(Built as designed: `project/accounts.py`, `account.js`, `friendsPopup.js`; see architecture.md and gameplay.md. Differences: the "sign in on another device" link was built, then removed on 2026-11-23 (it competed with the friend link; players sign in with their password); the confirmations use the browser's own dialog. Signing out clears the browser's stats, ✓s and daily games (2026-11-24), so someone else signing up on that browser starts empty.)*
Optional sign-in so history follows you across devices, and a scoreboard of your friends' results for today.

**Accounts**
- Username and password only: no email, so **no password reset**. The sign-up form says so plainly ("write it down"). Accounts are optional; everyone can still play without one.
- Passwords are stored salted and slowly hashed with werkzeug's `generate_password_hash` (already installed with Flask, so no new dependency), never in plain text. People reuse passwords, so this matters even though the game itself needs no protection. Logins are rate limited (per username and per IP) so passwords can't be guessed quickly.
- A signed-in device can show a "sign in on another device" link or QR code (single use, short-lived), so most people never have to type the password on a second device.
- On first sign-in, the stats already on this device (localStorage `stats`) move into the account, so history and today's results carry over.
- Archive solves are attached to the account too: the finished-puzzle list behind the 📅 calendar's ✓ marks (localStorage `archive`) moves in on first sign-in, and every archive puzzle finished while signed in is added. Every device you sign in on shows the same ✓s. They stay out of streaks, stats and the scoreboard, as now.

**Friends**
- Friendship is mutual only: two people are on each other's boards, or neither is. No requests, no following, no groups.
- Each player has one reusable friend link (like `constrainbow.com/friend/k7Qm2x`). Opening someone's link while signed in makes you friends at once. It can go in a group chat, and everyone who taps it becomes your friend. A "make a new link" button replaces the old link, in case it was posted somewhere public.
- Opening your own link, or one from someone who's already a friend, just shows a short note.
- The link is how new players arrive, so it must work for people who have never played: the page says something like "Danny wants you on their scoreboard", offers sign-up there, and finishes the friendship after it. It also lets them play today's puzzle first and sign up afterwards, keeping the pending friendship.
- Each friend's row has a 🗑 to remove them. It asks first ("Remove Sam? You'll both disappear from each other's boards") and breaks the friendship for both people. The other person isn't told. Becoming friends again takes a new link click.

**The scoreboard**
- A table: one row per person (you included), columns **SOLVE, SPEED, SCORE** in that order, each cell that person's result today in that mode. A friend who hasn't played that mode today shows `-`.
- **Hidden until you finish:** a column stays locked until you've finished that mode today. Your friends' cells there show 🔒, which looks different from `-` and invites you to play. Your own row always shows what you've finished. The server enforces this: it sends friends' results for a mode only once it has yours, so they can't be read with the browser's developer tools.
- **Cells:** SOLVE shows the time, plus `💡N` when there were reveals (`1:34`, `4:10 💡3`). SPEED shows the time. SCORE shows the day's best points so far (it updates if they improve, and is final once they press SHOW BEST; the same number as their share text).
- **Ranking:** SOLVE by fewer reveals, then faster time (reveals first keeps SOLVE about solving it yourself; time breaks ties, since about half of the no-reveal finishes in the logs so far took between 33 s and 3:48). SPEED by fastest time. SCORE by most points. A one-line note under the table explains the SOLVE rule.
- **Sorting:** tap a column header to sort by that mode. The default is the leftmost mode you've finished today, or alphabetical before you've finished any. A tapped sort isn't remembered between visits. People without a result in the sorted column go to the bottom, alphabetically.
- **Crown:** in each unlocked column, a 👑 goes next to the best result among the people on the board (worked out when the page shows it, not a global ranking). You can have it yourself. Ties share it (times are whole seconds; a SOLVE tie needs the same reveals and the same time). It appears only once at least two people have a result in that column, and never in a locked column.
- Only the daily puzzles count: archive puzzles, `/solve/day` and the SOLVE test batch don't, the same as streaks.
- **Tapping a result** opens a popup with that person's finished board (your own too), drawn like the game board, with their name and result on top. SOLVE: the answer board with their revealed tiles marked 💡 (everyone has the same words, so the interest is where they needed help). SPEED: their four words. SCORE: the board behind the result shown in the cell (their best so far today), with its points. A cell can only be tapped once its column is unlocked, so the popup never shows a solution before you've finished that mode. The server stores each result's final board (words, and SOLVE's revealed tiles) with it, and sends it only for unlocked columns.

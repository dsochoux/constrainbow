How to play CONSTRAINBOW (beta)

The object of the game is to come up with four five-letter English words (no plurals or past tense) that fit the colored constraints.

Tiles of the same color must be the same letter. Different colored tiles may not be the same letter. For example, if one orange is an F, all of the oranges must be F. The red and the orange tiles may not both be F.

For each puzzle, which comes out at 12:00am Eastern, there are many different solutions. In fact, this example puzzle has 23,062. You just need to find one as quickly as possible. Yours will look different than your friend's, so don't forget to compare!

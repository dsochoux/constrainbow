How to play CONSTRAINBOW

Your goal is to create four five-letter words, filling in the letters in any order that you wish. However, there are a couple of constraints:

Two tiles of different color (including black) may NEVER share a letter.

Colored tiles (excluding black) MUST all be the same letter.

Black tiles can repeat letters.

The keyboard will reflect what letters the currently selected tile can be.

You can, and most certainly will, change your board as much as you'd like. To select a tile, tap, click, or navigate with the arrow keys.

Good luck!
